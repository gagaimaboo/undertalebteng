DT Chapter 2: Even Stronger
====================================

Play: run DELTARUNE.exe in this folder. It goes straight into Even Stronger
(Chapter 2 modded). Start a NEW file.

This folder is separate from your normal DELTARUNE:
- it doesn't touch your real install,
- and it saves to %LOCALAPPDATA%\DELTARUNE_EVEN_STRONGER, so your real
  Chapter 2 saves are safe.

The "Chapter Select" button in the file menu doesn't go anywhere here
(there's no launcher in this folder).
